# v1.5.7 Conservative Live-DOM Fix

This build intentionally returns to the observed v1.5.3 visual/layout baseline and applies only the two facts proven by the live audit.

- Native player-name cosmetic buttons (`chat-name-*`) are excluded from generic Fantasy Skin button chrome.
- Skill level/XP readouts are detected as the live `<button>` they actually are, marked before button painting, excluded from action-button chrome, and flattened as data.
- The experimental player-HUD structural relayout is explicitly disabled. Fixing the username must not implicitly activate an unverified dormant HUD layout path.
- No audit overlay is included.

This is a rollback-and-isolate build, not a continuation of the v1.5.6 HUD rewrite.
